# Changelog

## Unreleased

- The Arena and the Medal Exchange are now in the item shop of every major town, not only Lohan.
- Dragon Stones: story bosses no longer drop them; Arena ×3 gives one, Elite battles in Cursed Zones 5% (was 30%), Medal Exchange price 12 (was 6).
- Soul Eater, Wraith Helm and Wraith Mail no longer drain HP.
- Fixed the "Cursed Zone" label sometimes disappearing while still in a cursed area.
- Source code and build script published.
- Attack items show their damage multiplier in the battle item list.
- The Divine Tree and the Moon are never cursed outside New Game+.
- Red-Eyed Dragon: STR 100% on all enemies (was 75%).
- New Game+: monsters scale with the game's own strength curve per area; drops come from the area of your level; armour forging.
- New Game+: a save created at "The End", carried-over characters and items, monsters scaled to the party's level, Hard values always on, stronger Elite battles and Cursed Zones, a fresh Arena, 14 EX additions and stronger Dragoon magic.
- The Arena entry fee scales with the boss in New Game+.

## 0.1.0 beta — 2026-11-01

First public release.

- Difficulty: Standard and Hard monster balance, reworked monster rewards and drops.
- Monster HP display with numbers and a coloured bar.
- Elite battles and Cursed Zones, with the Phantom Ship always cursed.
- The Forge: weapons +1 to +5 in every equipment shop, Forge Stones and Dragon Stones.
- The Arena of Lohan: re-fight defeated bosses scaled to your level, three grades, Heirloom weapons, Arena Medals and the Medal Exchange.
- New equipment: Wyrmling, Greater Wyrmling, Elder and Heirloom weapons, Busters, elemental weapons, new armour and accessories, the Seraph set.
- New items: Eternal Prayer, Glowmoss, Forge Stone, Dragon Stone, Arena Medal.
- Addition and Dragoon tweaks.
- Options for EXP, gold and addition EXP multipliers, Elite chance and Cursed Zones.
